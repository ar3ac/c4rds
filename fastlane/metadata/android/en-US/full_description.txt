A native, simple, lightning-fast and 100% offline-first Android application to manage your loyalty cards and barcodes (supermarkets, shops, gyms, clubs).

Features:
• Privacy-friendly: No account required, zero tracking, no data sent to external servers.
• Fast, targeted barcode and QR code scanner.
• Automatic 100% screen brightness on card detail view for effortless checkout scanning.
• Android App Shortcuts & Pinned Home shortcuts to open favorite cards instantly.
• Open JSON backup & restore (.c4rds).
• Easy card sharing via link or file.
• Custom reordering and clean Material 3 UI.
